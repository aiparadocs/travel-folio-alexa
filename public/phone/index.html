From a phrase to a trip

Plan at home. Keep the memories here.

Travel Folio is an Alexa+ experience simulation. It does not connect to an Echo or install an Alexa skill.

At home

Open the home planner to choose Kyoto or Seattle, review changes, and take a plan to your phone. The planner uses guided conversation by default; optional AI conversation and real weather need separate server settings.

On your phone

The QR link carries a copy of your itinerary. Add your own photos, words and stickers here. These memories stay in your browser and are not sent back to the home planner.

When you save a copy

A downloaded HTML journal keeps its own limited, offline planning demo. It works without the home server. Export your journal to keep photos and private notes before clearing browser storage.

Your journal

A small book for a big little trip.

Travel Folio is a working title. Plan at home, then keep photos and your own words in the same journal. English is the default; your own writing can be in any language.

What works

Manual itinerary editing, 1–14 days, visit checks, photos, captions, 32 stickers, packing lists, local saving, HTML and JSON backups, plan links and locally generated QR codes.

What is simulated

The conversation and Alexa launch are deterministic demonstrations. No live Alexa+, external AI, search, weather, transport check, reservation or payment is called. Starter itineraries are editable ideas, not recommendations checked against real places.

Photos and privacy

JPEG, PNG and WebP photos are resized on this device to 1,200px or less and redrawn without retaining original EXIF/GPS metadata. HEIC is not supported. Photos do not automatically mark places as visited.

Saving uses IndexedDB, with localStorage as a fallback. The app warns if neither works. Export backups: clearing browser data can remove a journal.

Microphone input may send audio to your browser’s speech provider, after you agree. Read-aloud uses a browser-provided voice; it is not Alexa. The web journal does not upload your photos.

Artwork

The original 20 stickers are preserved, plus 12 new travel designs. All are optimized WebP files embedded in this app. No Amazon logos, paper-handbook photos or third-party brand art are included.
